Spectrum

Let's science things up a bit.

Spectrum Science Club is the official student science society of Woxsen University's School of Sciences — a community of minds united by a shared passion for discovery across Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics, Biotechnology, Computer Science, Data Science & AI, and Agricultural Science.

We turn scientific curiosity into collective action through workshops, research showcases, outreach, hackathons, stargazing nights, and interdisciplinary projects.
